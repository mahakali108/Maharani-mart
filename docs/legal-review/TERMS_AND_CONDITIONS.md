# Terms and Conditions — review draft

**Version:** 0.1-review-draft
**Prepared:** 30 October 2026
**Status:** **UNPUBLISHED — subject to owner and Indian lawyer approval**

## 1. Parties and platform identity

These terms are proposed for the B2B wholesale platform made available under the trade/platform name **Maharani Traders** by **Ajay General**, a proprietorship operating from Gaya, Bihar, India. Before publication, insert the owner-approved principal business address, public support contact, GST registration status and GSTIN where applicable.

**Pending lawyer decision:** identify the contracting entity, seller of record and invoice issuer for (a) own-stock sales, (b) third-party seller sales and (c) mixed orders. Those roles must be shown to the buyer before order acceptance and consistently reflected in invoices, settlement records and policy text.

## 2. Intended B2B use and eligibility

The service is intended for authorised business purchasers purchasing products for legitimate business/resale use. A registrant must provide accurate account, shop, tax and delivery information and keep credentials confidential. Account approval, catalogue visibility, credit/Coins features and order placement may be restricted by the platform’s documented operational controls.

This intended B2B description does not remove rights that apply by law. Counsel must determine the treatment of each buyer/transaction under applicable consumer and e-commerce law.

## 3. Catalogue, products and sellers

The platform may display products held in Ajay General’s own stock and products offered by approved third-party marketplace sellers. For a marketplace line, the applicable seller identity, fulfilment responsibility, invoice issuer and customer-support route must be determined and displayed through approved operational flows.

Only cosmetics and FMCG products are intended to be listed. Food and food-related products are prohibited. Product images, packaging declarations, descriptions, stock and price are subject to verification at order time. A catalogue display, saved cart or quote is not a stock reservation or a binding offer until the authorised order workflow accepts it.

## 4. Prices, taxes and order acceptance

Prices, GST/tax treatment, pack size, minimum quantity, availability, discounts and account balance are revalidated by the server before order creation. The applicable tax rate, HSN, invoice particulars, place-of-supply treatment and GST invoice status must be determined from verified issuer data and CA-approved configuration.

Do not publish a document labelled “Tax Invoice” until the identified issuer’s required particulars are available and the applicable tax treatment is approved. The platform must not assume a marketplace TCS rate or deduct/settle TCS until a CA confirms applicability, responsibility and filing process.

An order is accepted only when the platform’s authorised server-side order workflow records it. The operator/seller may decline or cancel an order only in accordance with applicable law and approved operational rules, such as unavailable stock, pricing error, compliance hold, fraud prevention, non-serviceable address or other disclosed lawful grounds.

## 5. Delivery, title, risk and COD

Delivery is limited to owner-approved, active serviceable PIN codes and operationally supported addresses. Carrier, dispatch handling, delivery estimate, delivery charge, COD eligibility, collection authority, title transfer and risk transfer must be recorded and legally reviewed before publication. No delivery date, courier, service area, fee or COD promise is made by this draft.

For seller-fulfilled lines and split orders, responsibility for dispatch, customer communications, delivery proof, failed delivery and remittance must be assigned in the seller agreement and the relevant order records.

## 6. Cancellations, returns, shortages and refunds

Cancellation, return and refund availability depends on the real order status, the product, evidence, seller/issuer responsibility and applicable law. The final policy must identify the approved status-based rules for pending, confirmed, dispatched, delivered, refused, damaged, wrong, short and partially fulfilled orders. It must not promise an automatic approval, universal return window, charge or refund timeline unless the owner and lawyer approve the exact terms.

## 7. Account use, prohibited conduct and records

Users must not misuse the service, place fraudulent orders, submit false business/tax information, infringe rights, upload malicious material, bypass access controls or list prohibited/unapproved products. The platform may preserve records, restrict access and cooperate with lawful requests subject to applicable law and the approved privacy/retention policy.

## 8. Intellectual property and product claims

Brand names, packaging, product images and materials remain subject to the rights of their owners. Sellers must have authority to use submitted content and may not make misleading, counterfeit, unverified safety or regulatory claims. Product regulatory evidence is subject to the Product Authenticity and Anti-Counterfeit Policy.

## 9. Privacy and communications

Personal data is handled under the approved Privacy Policy. Account/order communications may be sent through configured channels for operational, security and support purposes. The final notice must state controller contact, purposes, retention, request handling and vendor/processor details.

## 10. Disputes, governing law and changes

The lawyer must supply approved governing-law, jurisdiction, dispute-resolution and notices wording after confirming the parties, transaction model and applicable law. These terms may be versioned and changed only through a controlled approval process; material changes must have an effective date and an appropriate notice/acceptance mechanism.

## Publication approvals required

- Owner: legal entity, address, contacts, business model and operating commitments.
- Lawyer: contract formation, consumer/e-commerce/intermediary applicability, liability, dispute, jurisdiction and policy consistency.
- CA: GST/invoice issuer, marketplace/TCS, settlement and credit-note treatment.
