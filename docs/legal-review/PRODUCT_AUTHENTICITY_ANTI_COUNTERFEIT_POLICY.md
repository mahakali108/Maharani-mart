# Product Authenticity and Anti-Counterfeit Policy — review draft

**Version:** 0.1-review-draft
**Prepared:** 30 October 2026
**Status:** **UNPUBLISHED — owner, product-compliance and lawyer review required**

## 1. Scope

This draft applies to cosmetics and FMCG products proposed for catalogue listing, whether supplied from Ajay General’s own stock or by a marketplace seller. Food and food-related products are outside the allowed platform scope and must not be listed, published or sold.

## 2. Source and authenticity evidence

Before a product is published or sold, the responsible owner/seller must provide applicable source and authenticity evidence, such as supplier invoice, purchase document, brand/distributor authorisation, manufacturer/importer details, label photographs and other records appropriate to the product. The compliance reviewer must record the evidence reference, applicable legal basis, reviewer, decision and review date.

Evidence submission alone does not make a product approved.

## 3. Cosmetics controls

For cosmetics, the reviewer must determine which requirements apply and obtain/record applicable manufacturer/importer identity and address, country of origin, manufacturing licence, import registration/authorisation, batch/lot, manufacturing/expiry/use-before data, net contents, ingredients, warnings, directions, label photographs and source documents. A missing/rejected applicable item prevents publication and sale until corrected and reviewed.

## 4. Packaged FMCG controls

For packaged FMCG goods, the reviewer must determine applicable Legal Metrology/label declarations and record evidence for MRP, selling price, net quantity/unit, pack size, manufacturer/packer/importer, country of origin where required, consumer-care contact, batch/expiry where applicable and label images. An unreviewed SKU must not bypass required declarations.

## 5. Review outcomes and controls

A product compliance record must be **pending**, **submitted**, **verified** or **rejected**, with reviewer, review date, evidence reference and applicable legal basis. Products with missing, pending, expired, rejected or unverifiable required evidence must remain unavailable for publication/sale. A material product, label, source, price, pack or compliance change may require re-review.

## 6. Takedown, incident and recall handling

The approved operations process must permit prompt listing suspension, stock hold, seller notice, customer/order review, complaint handling, evidence preservation and escalation for suspected counterfeit, expired, unsafe, recalled or misleading products. The final policy must not promise a recall/refund process unless the owner/legal team has approved and tested it.

## 7. Reporting and records

Reports of suspected counterfeit or unsafe products should be routed to the verified grievance/support process. Records must be access-controlled and retained under the approved privacy, accounting, product-safety and legal schedule.

## Publication approvals required

- Owner/product compliance reviewer: categories, evidence standards, reviewer authority and operational takedown process.
- Lawyer: consumer/product, cosmetics, Legal Metrology, liability and public-claim wording.
- Seller/brand owner: relevant source/authorisation evidence and contractual commitments.
