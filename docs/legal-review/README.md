# Maharani Traders legal review pack

**Version:** 0.1-review-draft
**Prepared:** 30 October 2026
**Publication status:** **UNPUBLISHED — owner, Indian lawyer and CA review required**

This pack is a technical drafting aid for **Ajay General**, trading through the platform name **Maharani Traders**. It is not legal advice, a legal certification, tax advice, an approval, or evidence that any document has been accepted by a seller, buyer, regulator, lawyer or CA.

## Non-negotiable publication controls

1. Do not publish a final policy until its document owner, effective date, version, business address, public contact details, grievance officer details and jurisdiction wording are approved in writing.
2. Do not insert an invented GSTIN, phone number, email address, bank detail, address, licence, delivery commitment, COD charge, return window, refund timeline, fee, TCS rate, or legal conclusion.
3. The current application launch gate must remain fail-closed until owner-recorded readiness approvals, an unexpired validity policy, and applicable legal/CA evidence are recorded.
4. The documents intentionally distinguish own-stock transactions from marketplace seller transactions. The lawyer and CA must determine the actual seller of record, invoice issuer, contractual flow, GST/TCS treatment, settlement responsibility and applicable consumer/intermediary rules for every transaction type.
5. The documents are written for a B2B wholesale/resale platform. A lawyer must confirm whether, and for which purchasers or transactions, consumer-law obligations apply despite that intended model.

## Required evidence register

The reviewer should maintain a controlled record outside public policy text for each publication decision:

- reviewer identity and role;
- evidence reference and storage location;
- review date and approval/rejection decision;
- effective date and next review/expiry date;
- document version and change summary;
- business owner sign-off; and
- lawyer/CA opinion reference where the decision concerns legal or tax treatment.

A filled form, uploaded file, URL, or unsigned draft is not approval by itself.

## Technical implementation boundaries

The repository contains public policy routes and an order launch readiness gate. A route existing in source is not publication approval. Similarly, an application workflow can capture evidence and audit changes, but cannot verify document authenticity, GST applicability, statutory classification, licence validity or legal compliance without qualified review.
