# Privacy Policy — review draft

**Version:** 0.1-review-draft
**Prepared:** 30 October 2026
**Status:** **UNPUBLISHED — subject to owner and Indian privacy-law review**

## 1. Controller and scope

This draft concerns the Maharani Traders B2B wholesale platform operated under the Ajay General business identity. Before publication, state the verified data-controller/business contact, principal address, privacy/grievance contact and the legal basis/applicability analysis approved by counsel.

India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act and Rules have staged commencement. This draft must not state that every DPDP obligation is already effective. Counsel must confirm the obligations effective for the deployment date and the relationship with the Information Technology Act/Rules and other applicable requirements.

## 2. Data categories

The service may process: account identity and login information; profile/role data; retailer/seller shop and business details; GST/tax details where supplied; addresses; cart/order/invoice/delivery/return records; Coins/ledger and collection records; support/grievance communications and attachments; seller offer, KYC and compliance evidence; device/session/security logs; and public-site analytics where configured.

Sensitive seller identity, PAN, bank/settlement and source documents must not be exposed through public catalogue, public policy pages or other sellers’ accounts.

## 3. Purposes

Data may be processed only for documented, applicable purposes such as account authentication, role/access control, retailer and seller onboarding, catalogue/product compliance review, quoting and order fulfilment, tax/invoice/settlement records, delivery and returns, customer support/grievance handling, fraud/security prevention, legal obligations, audit and service improvement. Final purpose/notice wording must be approved by counsel.

## 4. Sharing and processors

The operator must maintain a current processor/vendor register. The current technical architecture may involve Supabase for authentication/database/storage and Vercel for hosting/edge/analytics, but actual data flows, locations, contracts, subprocessors and cross-border transfers require owner/counsel verification before publication.

Data may be shared with the identified seller/fulfilling party only to the extent required for a relevant order, with service providers acting under approved controls, and with authorities where lawfully required.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Retention must be governed by an owner-approved record schedule. Orders, invoices, tax, financial ledger, fraud/security, grievance and contractual records can be subject to legal-retention exceptions and must not be automatically erased merely because an account is closed. The current application must not promise a universal deletion period.

The approved schedule must identify record type, purpose, retention period, legal basis/exception, archive/deletion method, system owner and review date.

## 6. Rights and requests

A verified individual may request access, correction, account assistance or deletion review through the approved privacy/support route. The workflow must record request reference, requester verification, category, assigned reviewer, action, decision, response and retention exception. No request is automatically fulfilled until identity, authority, affected records and legal retention requirements are assessed.

## 7. Security and incident response

The operator must maintain access control, least privilege, authentication/session protection, audit logging, backup/recovery, secure development, vendor review and incident-response procedures. A security incident must be assessed under the legal requirements effective at the time; notifications must be coordinated by the designated owner/legal process and must not be improvised in public policy text.

## 8. Cookies and analytics

The final notice must identify any non-essential analytics/cookies, their provider, purpose, retention and consent/choice mechanism where applicable. Public static asset/offline caching must not be described as caching private orders, addresses, API responses or authenticated sessions when it does not do so.

## 9. Changes and contact

The final published policy must display an approved effective date, version, change process and verified privacy/grievance contact. This draft contains no invented contact detail.

## Publication approvals required

- Owner: controller identity/contact, real systems/vendors, retention schedule, request operations.
- Lawyer/privacy adviser: DPDP/IT/e-commerce applicability, notice/consent/rights/cross-border wording.
- Security owner: tested incident, access, backup and vendor controls.
