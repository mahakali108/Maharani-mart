# Shipping and Delivery Policy — review draft

**Version:** 0.1-review-draft
**Prepared:** 30 October 2026
**Status:** **UNPUBLISHED — operational and legal approval required**

## 1. Serviceability

Maharani Traders is intended to accept online delivery orders only for addresses whose district, state and PIN code are recorded as active through the approved operational configuration. A registration area is not by itself delivery coverage. The final policy must identify the actual serviceability model without claiming universal Gaya, Bihar or India-wide coverage unless that is demonstrably true.

## 2. Order acceptance and dispatch

Order placement, acceptance, stock allocation, warehouse/seller assignment and dispatch occur under the recorded order workflow. The owner must approve the actual dispatch handling model, responsible party, carrier/courier arrangements, cut-off rules, estimated timelines, tracking process and customer communications before these are published.

No fixed dispatch/delivery period, courier or service fee is promised in this draft.

## 3. Own-stock, seller and mixed fulfilment

A transaction can involve own-stock products, a third-party seller’s products, or a mixed order. The final customer-facing flow and seller agreement must identify the fulfilling party, seller of record/invoice issuer where applicable, delivery responsibility, package/partial-delivery communication, support route and settlement responsibility for each line.

## 4. Charges and COD

Delivery charges, minimum order thresholds, COD eligibility, COD collection authority, collection receipts, remittance and failed-collection process require owner/CA/lawyer approval. The application must block checkout where a configured charge cannot be accurately included in the recorded order total. Do not publish a fee, free-delivery threshold or COD availability unless configured, tested and approved.

## 5. Delivery attempts, refusal and exceptions

The final policy must state the approved process for inaccurate addresses, non-serviceable PINs, failed contact, failed/returned delivery, refusal, partial delivery, shortage, damaged/wrong goods, delivery proof and re-dispatch. These are operational decisions and must not be invented by this draft.

## 6. Title, risk and records

Counsel must determine the approved title/risk-transfer wording for own-stock and marketplace transactions. Delivery status, proof, collection and exception records should be retained under the approved privacy, accounting and tax retention schedule.

## Publication approvals required

- Owner/operations: active PINs, fees, dispatch/carrier/COD process, exceptions and support model.
- Lawyer: title/risk, seller/fulfiller terms and customer disclosure.
- CA: COD/settlement/invoice implications, including marketplace flows.
