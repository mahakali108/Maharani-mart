# Marketplace Seller Agreement — review draft

**Version:** 0.1-review-draft
**Prepared:** 30 October 2026
**Status:** **UNPUBLISHED — must be converted to an executed lawyer-approved agreement**

## 1. Parties, authority and acceptance

This proposed agreement is between Ajay General, operating the Maharani Traders platform, and the identified marketplace seller. It becomes effective only after the seller entity, authorised signatory, version, acceptance method, effective date and applicable commercial schedules are verified and recorded. Invitation to the platform, account creation or document upload alone is not acceptance or activation.

## 2. Seller onboarding and KYC

Before activation, the seller must provide and keep current the applicable legal business name, entity/proprietor identity, principal business address, contact details, GST registration status/GSTIN where applicable, bank/settlement verification, authorised representative, source/product evidence and other legally required information. PAN, bank and private identity evidence must remain access-restricted and must not be public.

A seller remains pending/inactive until authorised Super Admin review records the required decision and evidence references. An inactive, rejected or suspended seller must not publish offers or fulfil marketplace orders.

## 3. Seller responsibilities

The seller represents that products, labels, packaging, source documents, licences, authorisations, tax details, marketing claims, stock and fulfilment information are lawful, accurate and current. The seller must provide invoice responsibility, return/refund responsibility, product-source evidence, brand/distributor authority, cosmetics/packaged-commodity documents where applicable, and anti-counterfeit undertaking.

The seller must cooperate with customer support, complaints, recalls, safety issues, tax/TCS review, settlement reconciliation, audit, regulator requests and dispute resolution. The final agreement must contain lawyer-approved warranties, indemnities, limitation terms and enforcement consequences.

## 4. Listings and compliance review

Seller offers may be created only for permitted cosmetics/FMCG products and remain subject to platform moderation. A product/offer cannot be published or sold until required product compliance is verified and any applicable reviewer/legal-basis record is complete. Price/MOQ/stock changes may require re-moderation. The platform may suspend a listing for regulatory, counterfeit, safety, tax, customer-protection or evidence concerns.

## 5. Seller of record, invoices and taxes

The owner, lawyer and CA must determine and document, per transaction model, the seller of record, contract party, invoice issuer, place-of-supply treatment, GST responsibility, credit-note/refund responsibility, marketplace TCS applicability, settlement deductions and filing-support records. This draft does not assume that Maharani Traders is an e-commerce operator for GST/TCS purposes or that a configurable percentage is legally correct.

## 6. Fulfilment, delivery, returns and settlement

The executed agreement must state fulfilment SLAs, inventory commitment, packaging, delivery handover, COD authority, failed/refused delivery, partial orders, proof, return pickup/inspection, refund payer, dispute handling, settlement cadence, reconciliation, payment method, fees/commission and reserves—only after commercial terms are approved. No rate, fee, settlement period or delivery promise is created by this draft.

## 7. Data and confidentiality

The seller may use buyer/order data only to fulfil and support its own authorised orders, must secure it, and must not use it for unrelated marketing or disclose it except as lawfully required. Seller access ends or is restricted when status/suspension changes, subject to legal retention and settlement needs.

## 8. Termination, suspension and records

The final agreement must define suspension/termination, takedown, outstanding orders, returns, settlements, data return/deletion, audit records, notice, dispute resolution and governing law. These provisions require lawyer review and seller acceptance.

## Execution checklist

- Seller entity/KYC and authorised signatory verified.
- Agreement version and commercial schedules approved.
- Seller-of-record/invoice/TCS responsibility approved by CA.
- Product/brand/source evidence reviewed.
- Acceptance time, method, IP/device/account record and reviewer recorded.
