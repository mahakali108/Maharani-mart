## Proposed declaration

The seller declares, subject to verification and final agreement execution, that:

1. it is duly authorised to offer the listed products and use submitted brand/content material;
2. all business, tax, address, contact and settlement information supplied is complete and current;
3. products are genuine, lawfully sourced, accurately described and supplied with applicable invoices/source records;
4. cosmetics have applicable manufacturer/importer, licence/registration, batch, expiry/use-before, ingredient, warning, direction, label and source evidence available for review;
5. packaged FMCG goods carry applicable declarations, including MRP, net quantity/unit, manufacturer/packer/importer, consumer-care, origin/batch/expiry details and label evidence where applicable;
6. food and food-related products will not be listed, offered or fulfilled through the platform;
7. it will not list counterfeit, unsafe, expired, recalled, adulterated, misleading or unapproved products;
8. it will maintain applicable invoice, GST, return/refund, delivery/COD, credit-note and tax/TCS cooperation records;
9. it will promptly cooperate with complaints, investigations, recalls, compliance corrections, seller moderation and audits; and
10. it accepts that unverified, rejected or suspended status prevents publication and fulfilment.
