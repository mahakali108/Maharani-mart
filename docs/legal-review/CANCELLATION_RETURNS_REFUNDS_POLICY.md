# Cancellation, Returns and Refund Policy — review draft

**Version:** 0.1-review-draft
**Prepared:** 30 October 2026
**Status:** **UNPUBLISHED — owner, operations, lawyer and CA review required**

## 1. Scope and no automatic promises

This draft applies to intended B2B wholesale orders placed through Maharani Traders, including own-stock, seller-fulfilled and mixed orders. It does not promise that every cancellation, return or refund will be accepted. The final policy must remain consistent with actual order-state controls, product condition, evidence, seller/invoice responsibility and applicable law.

## 2. Cancellation

The final policy must set approved cancellation eligibility by actual order status. Before dispatch/fulfilment, a cancellation request may be possible where the recorded order status and inventory/settlement controls permit it. After dispatch or delivery, cancellation may be unavailable and a return/issue process may instead apply. Existing pending orders are not automatically cancelled or blocked merely because a future launch-readiness configuration is incomplete; any change to that rule requires a separate owner decision.

For mixed orders, the policy must specify whether a cancellation can apply to one seller line, one sub-order or the entire parent order and how inventory, credits/Coins, charges, invoices and seller settlement are reconciled.

## 3. Delivery issues and evidence

The final operational workflow must permit reporting of damaged, wrong, short, missing or tampered deliveries. The approved policy must specify the permitted evidence (for example order reference, delivery evidence, package/label photographs or other verifiable record), reviewer, inspection route, and treatment of partial seller deliveries. It must not invent an evidence deadline or rejection rule.

## 4. Return eligibility

Product-specific safety, hygiene, regulatory, batch/expiry, resale condition and packaging issues can affect return eligibility. Cosmetics and packaged FMCG products must not be accepted/resold contrary to applicable safety or regulatory controls. The owner and lawyer must approve product-specific eligibility/exclusions and their legal basis before publication.

## 5. Refunds, credits and credit notes

The final policy must identify the party responsible for a refund or replacement, the approved payment method, applicable credit-note/invoice linkage, settlement effects, and any approved timeline. It must not state a fixed refund period without owner/CA/lawyer approval. Financial records must reconcile with the original order, collections, Coins/wallet ledger and seller settlement where applicable.

## 6. Escalation

An unresolved cancellation/return/refund issue may be raised through the approved support/grievance workflow. The final publication must include verified grievance details and legally reviewed escalation wording.

## Publication approvals required

- Owner/operations: actual state rules, evidence handling, inspection and replacement/refund process.
- Lawyer: B2B/consumer applicability, product exclusions and fairness wording.
- CA: issuer/refund/credit-note, GST and marketplace settlement treatment.
