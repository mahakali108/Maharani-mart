## Applicability: B2B is not an automatic blanket exemption

[Consumer Protection Act 2019 s.2(7)](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/18964/1/cpa.pdf) excludes goods bought for resale or a commercial purpose from the definition of consumer, with a livelihood/self-employment qualification for goods bought and **used** exclusively for that purpose. This does not simply erase resale from the exclusion. The [E-Commerce Rules 2020](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/theconsumerprotection/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules-2020) have their own scope; whether and which provisions apply to particular B2B transactions/services, downstream consumers and entity activities requires counsel. Product, GST, metrology, privacy, IT and contract obligations do not disappear merely because buyers are businesses. Avoid representing all consumer remedies as legal obligations for every pure resale transaction; offer transparent contractual support regardless. Review amendments and current gazette immediately before launch.
